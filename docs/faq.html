FAQ and Reading Guide

JABI.IO

What is The GenAI Briefing?

A free daily briefing that collects and ranks generative AI research papers, engineering posts, community discussions, podcasts and recorded talks, with short summaries and links to the original sources.

Who is it for?

Software engineers, ML practitioners, researchers and technical leaders who want to follow generative AI without reading dozens of feeds. It assumes technical familiarity and skips introductory explainers.

When is a new issue published?

Every day at about 06:45 Singapore time (22:45 UTC). Each issue has its own permanent page, and older issues are listed in the archive on the front page.

How do I read a Top 10 entry?

Each entry shows a category tag (Research, Engineering or Lab Post), the source, the date and a short summary. Below the summary are links to the original article and to the Hacker News discussion when one exists. Use the filter buttons above the list to show only one category.

What is the Reddit brief under each story?

A collapsible note summarizing how practitioners on communities such as r/LocalLLaMA, r/MachineLearning, r/ClaudeAI and r/OpenAI are discussing the topic, with links to search Reddit for the live threads. It is a reading aid, not a statement of fact.

How are stories chosen?

An automated agent gathers candidates from public sources, drops anything already featured in a previous issue, and ranks the rest by community reach and the substance of the summary, keeping a balance between research papers, engineering discussion and lab releases.

Are the summaries written by AI?

They are generated automatically from the source text and may be AI-assisted. They are short and always link to the original, so you can verify any claim. Corrections are welcome through the contact page.

Where can I find talks and podcasts?

The Tech Talks Index lists recent YouTube talks grouped by channel, and the Podcasts Index lists recent episodes of AI engineering shows. Both are linked in the navigation bar at the top of every page.

How do I follow the briefing?

Add the RSS feed at jabi.io/feed.xml to any feed reader. Email delivery is not open yet. You can also bookmark the front page, which always shows the latest issue.

Does the site show ads or track me?

The site may show clearly labeled ads served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies. Details and opt-out links are in the Privacy Policy. Your theme and font size choices are stored only in your browser.

Is the content free to reuse?

Headlines and short summaries are provided for reading and discussion. Original articles, papers and videos belong to their authors and are linked, not copied. See the Terms of Service for details.

How do I report an error or suggest a source?

Use the Contact page. Include the issue date and the link to the entry, and we will review it.
